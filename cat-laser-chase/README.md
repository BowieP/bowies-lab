# Cat Laser Chase 😺

Welcome to Cat Laser Chase, a fun, interactive browser game designed for my cat, Aggie! Or any cat really. It features a moving target that cats love to pounce on, with customizable settings to keep the game exciting.

---
![Aggie playing the cat game](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/BowieP/bowies-lab/refs/heads/main/cat-laser-chase/aggieplayingcatgame.jpeg)

## Features 🎮
* Interactive Gameplay: Click or tap the red dot to score points!
* Customization: Use the settings menu to adjust:
  * Dot Color: Pick your cat's favorite color.
  * Dot Size: Make it bigger or smaller.
  * Speed: Adjust how fast the dot zips around the screen.
* Score Tracker: Keep track of how many times your cat successfully "catches" the laser.

## How to Play 🚀
1. Download and open the cat-laser-chase.html file in any modern web browser.
2. Click the Settings button to adjust the dot's size, speed, and color to suit your cat's playstyle.
3. Watch your cat try to catch the dot! Every tap on the dot increases your score in the top-left corner.
